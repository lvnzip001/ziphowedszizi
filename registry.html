FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What should I wear?

Formal attire in soft neutrals — cream, champagne, and beige tones are welcome. We only ask that you avoid all-white, out of respect for the bride.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones, but to help us keep the evening intimate, we've decided to make our celebration an adults-only affair. We hope this gives you a wonderful night off to celebrate with us.

Can I bring a plus one?

We've kept our guest list intimate, so invitations are extended to those named on the invite. If you have any questions about yours, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, on-site parking is available for all guests. We recommend arriving at least 20–30 minutes before the ceremony start time to allow time to park and be seated.

Who do I contact with questions?

For any questions on the day, please reach out to either of us directly, or to a member of our wedding party — our contact details are on your invitation.

By when should I RSVP?

We kindly ask that you RSVP by 25 January 2027 so we can finalise catering and seating. You can let us know right here on our RSVP page.
