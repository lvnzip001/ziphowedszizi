Dress code

What to wear

Formal attire

Soft neutrals & warm tones

We'd love for you to dress formally, in soft neutrals — think cream, champagne, beige and warm tan, echoing the palette of our own wedding. We only ask that you please avoid all-white, out of respect for the bride.

A note on comfort: the celebration will move between indoor and outdoor moments, so breathable fabrics and comfortable shoes are worth considering.

Please avoid

All-white or ivory outfits (reserved for the bride), and very casual wear like shorts, flip-flops or sneakers.

Feel free to wear

Suits, dresses, and formal separates in the palette above — florals and subtle patterns are welcome too.
