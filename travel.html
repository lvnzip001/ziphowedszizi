Travel & accommodation

Travel & Accommodation Details

Everything our out-of-town guests need to plan their trip.

Getting there

Flying: King Shaka International Airport is about 1.5 hours from Brahman Hills by car.

From Johannesburg: Take the N3 toward Durban, use Exit 132 and follow the Brahman Hills signs.

From the Eastern Cape: Travel via Kokstad, Underberg and Howick, then join the N3 north to Exit 132.

Where to stay

Brahman Hills itself offers on-site hotel rooms and self-catering villas — a lovely option if you'd like to stay right where the celebration is. We'll add a shortlist of nearby Midlands Meander accommodation here too.

Getting around

The venue is rural, so a car is recommended — on-site parking is available. Notes on shuttles or rideshare availability to follow.
