FAQs

Good to know

What time should I arrive for the ceremony?

Please arrive at Brahman Hills promptly at 12:30 to allow ample time for parking and getting seated by 12:45 before the procession begins at 13:00.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We are having an unplugged ceremony. Once the ceremony begins, we ask that all phones be silenced and put away. We would like our guests to be fully present in the moment with us. We have hired the best photographer and videographer to capture our day and our guests. Photos and videos will be allowed at the cocktail hour and reception.

What should I wear?

Formal attire in our Mocha Blush palette is encouraged: dark chocolate, chocolate brown, cocoa brown, taupe beige, dusty rose and blush pink. Please avoid white or ivory, which are reserved for the bride. See the full Dress Code page for more.

Are kids welcome?

We love your little ones, but to help us keep the evening intimate, we've decided to make our celebration an adults-only affair. We hope this gives you a wonderful night off to celebrate with us.

Can I bring a plus one?

We've kept our guest list intimate, so invitations are extended to those named on the invite. If you have any questions about yours, please don't hesitate to reach out to us directly.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, on-site parking is available for all guests. We recommend arriving at least 20–30 minutes before the ceremony start time to allow time to park and be seated.

Who do I contact with questions?

For any questions on the day, please reach out to either of us directly, or to a member of our wedding party — our contact details are on your invitation.

By when should I RSVP?

We kindly ask that you RSVP by 25 January 2027 so we can finalise catering and seating. You can let us know right here on our RSVP page.

Where can I find travel & accommodation info?

Everything for out-of-town guests — flights, hotels, and getting around — is on our Travel & Accommodation page.
